How this works

Everything runs in cycles of three. A probe quiz goes past what you can do yet, to find where it gets hard. A lesson covers exactly what the probe caught, and nothing you already had. A check quiz then uses the same shapes with different numbers, to see whether it stuck. Anything still loose comes back in the next cycle, taught a different way.
