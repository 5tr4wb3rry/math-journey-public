Mathematical journey

Algebra to calculus, the long way round

An assessment goes past what you can do yet, to find which topics get hard. Each of those topics gets a lesson, and each lesson gets a check — the same shapes with different numbers, to see whether it stuck. Once every topic has passed its check, a unit assessment covers the whole unit and everything before it.
