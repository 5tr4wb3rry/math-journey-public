What a unit is

The syllabus is the whole territory: every topic, in no particular order. A unit is the working batch — a named set of those topics taken on together. It opens with an assessment that finds which of them are missing, each missing topic gets its own lesson and check, and once every one of those checks has been passed the unit closes with a unit assessment covering it and everything before it.

A topic can only be in one unit at a time, and topics with no unit are simply not being worked on yet — that is the entire relationship between this tab and the syllabus. The Unit column over there names the unit each topic belongs to.
