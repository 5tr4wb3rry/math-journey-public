When the sign flips

You already turn a sum and a product into a² + b². This is the same move with a minus in it, and the only thing that changes is which way 2ab goes.

The one you know

a + b = 8,  ab = 3.   Find a² + b².

(a + b)² = a² + 2ab + b²
    64   = a² + b² + 6
a² + b² = 58

Nothing there needs a or b. That is the whole appeal of the move.

Now with a minus

a − b = 3,  ab = 2.   Find a² + b².

Square what you were given, exactly as before:

(a − b)² = a² − 2ab + b²
     9   = a² + b² − 4

And here is the step worth slowing down on. The 2ab is being taken away on the right, so to get a² + b² on its own you add it back: 

a² + b² = 9 + 4 = 13
The tell Plus version: subtract 2ab. Minus version: add it. The sign you apply is always the opposite of the sign in the bracket, because you are undoing what the squaring did.

Why the opposite, every time

Write the two expansions next to each other and it stops being a rule to remember:

(a + b)² = a² + b²  PLUS  2ab   → so a² + b² = (a+b)² − 2ab
(a − b)² = a² + b²  MINUS 2ab   → so a² + b² = (a−b)² + 2ab

In both lines a² + b² is the thing you want and 2ab is the passenger. You move the passenger to the other side, and moving it flips its sign. That is all.

A quick sanity check that costs nothing: a² + b² can never be negative, and it is never smaller than 2ab. If your answer comes out below 2ab, the sign went the wrong way.

One more, worked

a − b = 5,  ab = 6.   Find a² + b².

(a − b)² = a² + b² − 2ab
    25   = a² + b² − 12
a² + b² = 25 + 12 = 37

Check it against the sanity rule: 2ab is 12, and 37 is comfortably bigger. Good.

Practice

  1. a − b = 4, ab = 3. Find a² + b².
    Answer

    16 + 6 = 22.

  2. a + b = 7, ab = 5. Find a² + b².
    Answer

    Plus version, so subtract: 49 − 10 = 39.

  3. a − b = 1, ab = 12. Find a² + b².
    Answer

    1 + 24 = 25. Small bracket, large passenger — the answer is almost all 2ab.

  4. a − b = 10, ab = 0. Find a² + b².
    Answer

    100 + 0 = 100. If ab = 0 then one of them is 0, so the two versions agree — the only case where the sign cannot catch you out.
