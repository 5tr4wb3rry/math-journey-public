Covered = met it and can use it. Mastered = all three agree: the record shows it used correctly and unprompted on a problem that didn't announce it, the parent agrees, and he says he owns it. Any one of the three saying no keeps it at covered — his no is decisive, and a topic he doesn't feel he owns is not mastered no matter what the score said.
